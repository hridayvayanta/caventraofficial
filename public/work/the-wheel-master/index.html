Client & Context

The Wheel Master · Automotive Refurbishment (Sanathal Circle, Ahmedabad, Gujarat)

Project Overview

Managing an alloy wheel repair workshop involves handling customer details, repair orders, job statuses, inventory, billing, insurance-related work, and deliveries. Maintaining these records manually or across separate systems makes information difficult to organize, retrieve, and update efficiently.\n\nWe developed a web-based workshop management application tailored to The Wheel Master's operational needs. The application brings essential business records and workflows into a centralized interface, helping staff organize customer information, track repair jobs, manage invoices and inventory, and monitor related business activities.

The Business Challenge

Scattered paper-based job cards, disjointed repair status tracking, manual billing reconciliation, and lack of real-time inventory visibility across technicians and front-desk staff.

Engineered Solution

Caventra engineered a unified digital workshop management application featuring centralized customer records, job card workflows, inventory stock monitoring, PDF invoice generation, insurance tracking, and visual analytics dashboards.

Key Features & Technical Capabilities

  • Dashboard — Centralized overview of active workshop operations and job metrics\nCustomer Management — Organize and maintain comprehensive customer profiles and histories\nRepair Job Management — Record repair orders, monitor wheel refurbishment stages, and track job progress\nInventory Management — Maintain real-time workshop consumable and spare parts stock records\nInvoice Management — Prepare and manage billing records with instant PDF export via jsPDF\nInsurance Management — Organize insurance-related repair claims and documentation\nCourier Tracking — Maintain shipment, dispatch, and delivery tracking information\nReports & Analytics — Visual business analytics powered by Chart.js and Excel export via SheetJS

Tangible Results & Current Status

Created a centralized digital interface for workshop management, structured customer and billing records, eliminated reliance on manual paper records, and established the foundation for future server-backed database expansions. Location: Sanathal Circle, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
