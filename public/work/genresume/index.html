Project Overview

Creating an effective resume can be challenging when users lack professional templates, structured guidance, or an organized way to present their qualifications. GenResume aims to simplify this process by providing a centralized platform for building and managing resumes.\n\nThe platform focuses on making resume creation more accessible while helping users structure their personal information, education, skills, and professional experience into a clear format suitable for career opportunities.

The Business Challenge

Job seekers frequently struggle with inconsistent formatting, unstructured qualification sections, and lack of browser-based version control when tailoring applications for diverse roles.

Engineered Solution

A modular, step-by-step resume editor with live layout previews, structured data schema, automated section alignment, and instant PDF export capabilities.

Key Features & Technical Capabilities

  • Resume Creation — Organize personal information, education, skills, and experience\nStructured Resume Sections — Present career information in a consistent, readable format\nDigital Resume Management — Manage multiple resume versions in a centralized web app\nProfessional Presentation — Structure content to improve clarity, visual hierarchy, and ATS readability\nWeb-Based Accessibility — Access resume-building functionality across desktop and mobile browsers

Tangible Results & Current Status

Empowered individuals to present their career achievements with clarity, structure, and confidence.
