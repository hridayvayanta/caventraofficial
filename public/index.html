About Caventra · Verified October 2026

Engineering-First Digital Studio

Caventra is an engineering-first digital studio based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We design, build, host, secure, and manage custom websites and digital software for businesses worldwide with bespoke hand-crafted code, zero page-builders, sub-second page performance, and 100% intellectual property ownership.

Custom web development is the engineering practice of hand-crafting semantic HTML, modern CSS, and clean JavaScript tailored to business needs. Unlike template agencies that rely on fragile WordPress plugins, Caventra operates as a dedicated technical partner.

Managed website hosting is a high-speed cloud infrastructure service featuring global Content Delivery Network edge caching, automated SSL encryption, and continuous uptime monitoring. Website security refers to enterprise HTTP headers and proactive firewall protections to keep customer data safe.

Continuous website maintenance is a proactive software engineering service providing real-time uptime monitoring, weekly security updates, and regular off-site backups. Brand identity design is the strategic development of visual identity systems, typography guidelines, and design systems for ambitious companies.

Disciplined Delivery

How We Deliver Custom Websites: Seven-Stage Process

Our disciplined seven-stage delivery process guides your custom website project smoothly from initial strategic discovery all the way to high-speed global cloud deployment.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Requirements

    We discuss your vision, goals, target audience, and project scope. Our team defines core requirements and success metrics to ensure your new website delivers real commercial growth.

  2. 02

    Planning & Architecture

    We plan site structure, wireframe flows, tech stack, and clear milestones. You receive an upfront architecture blueprint and an itemized fixed quote before any coding begins.

  3. 03

    Design & Prototyping

    We craft intentional layouts and responsive prototypes for your review. Our interactive designs look sharp in both dark and light modes across phones, tablets, and desktop viewports.

  4. 04

    Full-Stack Engineering

    We write clean, lightweight code without fragile page-builders. Every component is engineered by hand for fast loading, semantic markup, and reliable cross-browser stability.

  5. 05

    Testing & Verification

    We test across phones, tablets, and desktops for speed, forms, and accessibility. Rigorous quality assurance guarantees high Core Web Vitals scores and smooth user journeys.

  6. 06

    Launch & Deployment

    We handle domain configuration, SSL, and zero-downtime global deployment. Your website launches seamlessly on high-speed cloud edge networks with automated security certificates.

  7. 07

    Ongoing Support & Scaling

    We provide ongoing maintenance, security updates, and performance tuning. Our engineering team monitors your live site continuously so it remains fast, protected, and up to date.

Client Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Read straightforward answers to common questions about our custom web engineering, intellectual property ownership, delivery timelines, pricing, and ongoing website management services.

What services does Caventra provide?

Caventra builds, hosts, protects, and manages fast custom websites and digital software for growing businesses. Our four core services cover complete website design and development, reliable cloud hosting with continuous uptime, proactive site security, and monthly care to keep your site fast, fresh, and bug-free at all times.

Do I own my code and website after launch?

Yes, you own 100 percent of your code and digital assets. When your project is complete and paid for, we transfer full intellectual property ownership directly to you. That includes all source code files, design files, live hosting keys, and site accounts with zero vendor lock-in or recurring platform fees.

How does Caventra differ from traditional agencies?

Caventra writes clean custom code from scratch rather than relying on slow templates like WordPress or Wix. Your site loads faster, looks truly unique, and will not break from plugin conflicts. You also work directly with our founding engineers who build your product, not non-technical sales reps or middle managers.

How long does a typical project take to complete?

A standard custom business website takes two to three weeks from kickoff to launch. More complex web apps, member portals, and online store platforms take four to six weeks. We work through seven clear stages with live weekly preview links so you always see exactly what is being built.

How much does a custom website cost with Caventra?

Custom business websites range from 850 to 2,800 dollars depending on your page count, custom features, and backend requirements. We provide upfront itemized pricing with clear milestones and no hidden fees. You can configure your project requirements and receive an instant estimate with our free Cost Estimator tool.

Can you maintain and support a website you did not build?

Yes, we can manage, host, and speed up websites built by others. Through our website management service, our engineers review your existing site code, fix urgent bugs, improve load speed, set up daily backups, and install security protections so your site stays fast, safe, and up to date.
