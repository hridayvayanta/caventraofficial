Client Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you evaluate partnering with Caventra.

Do I own my code and website after launch?

Yes, 100%. Once final payment is fulfilled, all source code, design assets, and deployment keys transfer directly to you.

How does Caventra differ from traditional agencies?

We write custom, lightweight code instead of using slow templates like WordPress or Wix. Your site loads faster, looks unique, and doesn't break.

How long does a typical project take to complete?

Standard business websites take 2 to 3 weeks. Custom web apps and complex platforms take 4 to 6 weeks.

Can you maintain and support a website you didn't build?

Yes. Through our MANAGE service, we audit your existing site, resolve urgent issues, and provide regular maintenance and hosting.
