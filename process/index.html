Our 7-Stage Process

A disciplined engineering methodology from initial discovery to continuous post-launch support.

Every project at Caventra is engineered through seven transparent stages. We eliminate ambiguity through upfront milestone scoping, clean architecture, and direct collaboration with our engineering team.

01

Discovery & Requirements

We analyze your business model, target audience, core commercial objectives, and technical constraints to establish a focused project brief.

02

Planning & Architecture

We design data models, URL structures, tech stack choices, milestone deliverables, and upfront transparent pricing.

03

UI/UX Design & Prototyping

We craft purposeful typography, responsive grid layouts, and interactive wireframes in both dark and light modes for client review.

04

Full-Stack Web Engineering

We write clean, modular, hand-crafted code with zero fragile page-builders, sub-second load times, and responsive fluid design.

05

Quality Testing & Verification

Cross-device browser testing, accessibility audits, form submission checks, Core Web Vitals optimization, and client sign-off.

06

Launch & Production Deployment

Zero-downtime release to global CDN networks, domain/DNS configuration, SSL provisioning, and sitemap registration.

07

Ongoing Support & Scaling

Post-launch monitoring, proactive software updates, security patches, and continuous iterative improvements as your business expands.
