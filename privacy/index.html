Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Caventra ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle, collect, protect, and process data when you interact with our website and digital studio services.

1. Data Controller

Caventra is the data controller for information processed through this website. For any inquiries regarding your data, contact us at caventraofficial@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect & Purpose

We adhere to strict data minimization. We only collect information that you knowingly submit via our contact form:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email, company, request type, budget, and project brief.
  • Purpose: To review project proposals, provide estimates, and deliver custom digital solutions.
  • Zero Tracking SDKs: We do not track your activity across websites, and we do not use third-party behavioral analytics or advertising trackers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on your explicit consent, pre-contractual discussions, and our legitimate business interest in responding to client inquiries.

4. Age Restriction & Protection of Minors

Our website and software services are intended strictly for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors. If personal data of a minor is submitted, we will permanently delete it upon notice.

5. Security & Third-Party Audit

Our infrastructure undergoes strict security controls, including parameterized database operations, cryptographic session tokens, secure HTTPS transport, and strict Content Security Policies (CSP). We do not embed untrusted third-party SDKs.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Option

We retain inquiry information only as long as necessary to address your project or satisfy legal requirements.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion Request

You have the right to access, correct, or permanently delete your personal information from our systems at any time.

To request data deletion, select "Data Deletion Request" in our Contact Form or email caventraofficial@gmail.com. We will confirm deletion within 30 days.
