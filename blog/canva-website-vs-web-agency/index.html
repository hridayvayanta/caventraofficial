Canva Website vs Web Agency: Which Should You Choose?

Canva is fine for simple pages. Here is when a business needs a professionally built website instead.

V
Ved Nimbark

Head of Design at Caventra

Canva is a popular visual design tool that recently introduced the ability to publish lightweight web pages. For temporary events or school projects, it can be convenient. However, for a commercial enterprise seeking paying clients, its technical limitations quickly become apparent.

When a Canva Website Works

  • You need a quick, temporary single-page placeholder launched in an afternoon.
  • Your project has zero budget and minimal expectations.
  • You are comfortable manually designing, formatting, and updating every element yourself.
  • The page does not require search rankings, dynamic databases, or automated lead capture.

Where the Limits Show

  • Poor Technical SEO & Indexing: Canva renders text and layouts through heavy, non-semantic DOM structures that search engines struggle to index effectively.
  • Zero Custom Functionality: No support for server-side databases, custom calculators, client portals, or tailored booking flows.
  • Slow Mobile Performance: Canva pages often package bloated vector scripts, resulting in sluggish mobile load times and poor Core Web Vitals.
  • Platform Dependency: Your digital asset is hosted within Canva’s walled garden—you cannot export the source code or migrate to your own hosting server.

When a Web Agency Makes Sense

If your website is expected to generate client inquiries, establish brand authority, rank on Google, and scale alongside your business revenue, a bespoke build by a dedicated agency is an essential investment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Canva website good enough for a business?

For a very simple site, sometimes. For growth, SEO and custom features, a professionally built site gives more control.

What is the difference between Canva and a web agency?

Canva is a tool you use yourself. A web agency is a team that builds and looks after the site for you.
