Project Overview

Building projects involve multiple stakeholders, complex workflows, and information spread across disconnected tools. PLINTH is designed to bring these moving parts together through a centralized platform that simplifies collaboration, improves project visibility, and supports informed decision-making throughout the building lifecycle.\n\nBy combining AI-assisted design capabilities with project management, cost estimation, document management, and construction tracking, PLINTH creates a connected environment for everyone involved in bringing a building from concept to completion.

The Business Challenge

Traditional construction projects suffer from fragmented stakeholder silos, disconnected spreadsheets, slow manual quantity estimation, and lack of real-time site synchronization between architects, engineers, contractors, and vendors.

Engineered Solution

Caventra engineered a unified digital ecosystem integrating AI plot analysis, preliminary floor-plan generation, automated BOQ calculations, WebSocket-powered real-time task updates, and centralized role-based document approvals.

Key Features & Technical Capabilities

  • AI-Assisted Design — Plot analysis, preliminary floor-plan generation, and design alternatives\n3D Visualization & Interior Design — Visual exploration of architectural concepts, materials, and layouts\nEngineering Assistance — Preliminary structural assistance and compliance-related checks\nCost Estimation & BOQ — Structured quantity takeoffs, cost estimates, and budget visibility\nProject & Construction Management — Tasks, milestones, progress tracking, and site reporting\nDocument & Approval Management — Centralized project files, revisions, reviews, and approvals\nStakeholder Collaboration — Role-based workflows for clients, architects, engineers, contractors, and vendors

Tangible Results & Current Status
